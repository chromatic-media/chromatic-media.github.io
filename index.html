Welcome, traveler

Here you will find small, friendly games and programs made for the people in our lives: big clear buttons, no ads, nothing to sign up for. Everything on these pages is free to take and keep.

Many of them were made for folks with special needs, and for the people who look after them. If one of them makes someone's day a little easier, it has done its job.

The Scriptorium

Chromatic Media is one person making free programs in spare hours, late at night, by candlelight more often than not. These are the vows every one of them keeps.

No ads

Nothing to skip, nothing to close, nothing for sale.

No accounts

No sign-up, no password, no email to remember.

Nothing collected

What happens on your device stays on your device.

Made with care

Big buttons, clear words, and no way to get stuck.

Send a raven

Found a bug, have an idea, or want to tell us how one of these is working for someone you care about? Owls, ravens and plain old email all reach the same desk.
