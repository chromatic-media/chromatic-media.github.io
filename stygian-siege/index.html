Rules of engagement

Download.
Open. Fire.

No installer, no account, no launcher. One file, and the battle is on. Settings are kept between builds.

If Windows shows a blue box that says "Windows protected your PC": that only means the game isn't from a big company. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  1. Download the game

    Use the gold button above. The file is called Stygian-Siege-V3R6-Windows.exe. The download page also has a zip with the What's new notes.

  2. Open the file

    Double-click it. The title screen appears: a dusk battlefield with two guns trading shells. Press HOW TO PLAY first if you like.

  3. Set up a battle

    Press PLAY for the War Room: pick a landscape, an opponent from Easy to Insane, and a flag. Then START BATTLE.

  4. Aim and fire

    Tap a unit, drag anywhere on the field (direction is the angle, length is the power), check the wind sock, press FIRE. Anything you knock into the sea is gone for good.

The island

Everything else that wants you dead

Six landscapes

  • Plains, desert, arctic, canyon, mountain and volcanic.
  • Every one of them blows apart, shell by shell.
  • Sea at both edges. On the canyon map the gap in the middle is water too. On the volcanic map it's lava, which is not better.
PlainsDesertArcticCanyonMountainVolcanic

Hazards & neighbours

  • Oil drums chain-explode. Mines go off when anything lands near them.
  • Neutral buildings sit between the squads and change the game: wreck the enemy Ammo Dump to take their special shells, their Radar to blind them to the wind, their Chow Hall to shake their aim. Hit the Barracks to repair your weakest unit.
  • Hit your own and the enemy gets the benefit.

The clock & the opponent

  • Sudden death: after twelve rounds the water starts rising every turn. Anything it reaches is lost. Finish it.
  • The computer opponent learns from its misses, and reads the wind from Medium up.
  • Turn timer, wind mode, squad size and classic-hits rule are all yours to set.

Ask the quartermaster

Questions from the ranks

Windows says it "protected my PC". Did I download something bad?

No. That blue box turns up for any program that isn't signed by a big company, and we're a small one. Click More info, then Run anyway. If you'd like to check the file first, its SHA-256 fingerprint is on the download page.

Is it really free? What's the catch?

Free, no ads, no account, nothing collected, nothing phones home. The catch is that the quartermaster will not help you aim.

Is there an Android or Mac version?

Windows only for now. An Android version is on the list, along with two players on one screen and a training range. Mac: not planned, no Mac here to test on.

What does "still in development" mean?

The game is complete and playable from the title screen to the victory show, and every build is put through hundreds of automated battles before it ships. It means more is coming, and that a rough edge here and there is possible. Tell us about those at support@chromaticmedia.us.

How do I aim? My shots keep going into the sea.

Drag anywhere on the battlefield: the direction sets the barrel angle, the length sets the power. Nothing fires until you press FIRE. Then read the wind sock at the top: wind bends every shot. A-, A+, P- and P+ nudge your aim by one. Easy opponents miss a lot too; it evens out.

My bunker won't move. Is it stuck?

It's dug in. The bunker never moves, not for a shell, not for a nuke. That's its whole personality. Use Teleport if you must relocate it.

How do I remove the game?

Delete the file. Nothing was installed. Its settings live in a small folder under your Windows user profile, and they're harmless to leave.

Is it made with AI?

Yes, and proudly: built by Chromatic Media with the help of AI (Claude) for the code, the art (every picture in the game is drawn by its own code) and the sound effects. Fonts are Black Ops One and Rajdhani under the SIL Open Font License, included with the game.
